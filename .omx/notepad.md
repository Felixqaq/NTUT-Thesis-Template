### NTUT thesis formatting rules from `學校論文規範/J1.pdf`

- Order: cover, blank page, title page, oral defense approval sheet, Chinese abstract, English abstract, acknowledgements, TOC, list of tables, list of figures, body, references, appendices, symbol/formula glossary.
- Abstract: summarize problem and results; <= 500 Chinese characters or one page; no references, figure citations, or table citations.
- Body: A4 portrait white paper, 80 lb and double-sided in principle; Chinese font DFKai/標楷體, English and numbers Times New Roman; body 12pt, thesis title 24pt, chapter title 20pt, section title 18pt; 1.5 line spacing; Chinese paragraph first-line indent two characters.
- Margins: left/right/top 2.5 cm, bottom 2.75 cm. Page number bottom center, at least 1.5 cm from bottom edge; no "page", hyphens, or extra symbols around page numbers.
- Page numbers: title page and approval sheet no page number; front matter lowercase roman; body and back matter arabic.
- Chapter title: new page, centered, 20pt bold DFKai, no punctuation or English translation. Section titles: left-aligned, 18pt bold DFKai, one blank line before and no blank line after, no punctuation or English translation.
- TOC: include front matter and chapter/section starts, generally at least first-level sections. List of tables/figures may be omitted if only one or two tables/figures.
- Figures/tables: cite as "如圖 X 所示" or "如表 X 所示", not "如下圖/下表"; table captions above, figure captions below; Chinese captions no period; captions concise and no abbreviations.
- Equations: numbered by chapter `(章.序)`, centered with number right-aligned; equations should carry punctuation and use italic math symbols.
- References are required; in-text citations use square brackets unless advisor/field style overrides. Appendices hold long supporting material such as algorithms, code, questionnaires, symbols, and formulas.
